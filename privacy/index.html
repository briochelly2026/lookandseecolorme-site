Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short: the Color Me! Paint app collects no personal data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Drawings stay on the device.

This policy covers the Color Me! Paint app (on Google Play and the App Store, and at lookandseecolorme.com/paint) and the website lookandseecolorme.com, run by the authors of the Look & See, Color Me! books ("we").

The Color Me! Paint app

  • No personal information. The app does not ask for names, email addresses, photos, location, contacts or any other personal information, and it has no sign-up.
  • No ads and no tracking. The app shows no advertising and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • Drawings stay on the device. Pictures in progress and the "Can You Find Me?" progress are saved only in the app's storage on the device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Saving and printing. When a parent saves or prints a drawing, the file goes to the device's own photos, files or printer. Nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Purchases. Extra drawings are sold as one-time in-app purchases handled entirely by Google Play or Apple. We never see payment details. The store only tells the app which books were bought, so they can be unlocked and restored.
  • Parental gate. Purchases sit behind a question for grown-ups, so children cannot buy by accident.
  • No external links in the app that take children out of it.

Children

The app is made for families and is safe for children to use. Because it collects no personal information at all, it collects none from children. This follows the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU and UK GDPR, and the Google Play Families and Apple Kids rules.

The website

The website is a static site. It counts page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly tool that uses no cookies and stores no personal data. If you choose to send us your email address (for example, in the quiz or to get free pages), we use it only to send what you asked for, never sell it, and delete it when you ask.

Store and service providers

Downloads and purchases are handled by Google Play and the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its fonts from Google Fonts. The app itself makes no network requests except to the store for purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
