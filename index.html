How it all began

A book born at the kitchen table

Look & See, Color Me! started with afternoons of painting with Mia, a little girl who loves every color she can imagine. Each book is made for that same moment: a grown-up and a child, side by side, filling a page with color.

“The magic isn't in staying inside the lines. It's in filling life with color.”

With love, Mia & Mommy
