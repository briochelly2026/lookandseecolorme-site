Privacy & Photo Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Look & See, Color Me! makes coloring books for kids of all ages. Many of our readers are children, so we keep this simple: we collect as little as possible, we never sell anything about you, and we treat every child's photo and artwork with care.

For kids: This website is for coloring fun. Please don't type your name, address or any secret information anywhere. If you want to share your drawing, ask a grown-up to do it for you.

What this website collects

  • We do not ask for names, emails, addresses or any personal information to download stickers or coloring pages.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • Like any website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and our font provider (Google Fonts) receive basic technical data such as your IP address when the page loads, to deliver the page. We do not use this data to identify you.
  • If we add an email sign-up in the future, it will be for parents only, it will say clearly what you get, and you will be able to unsubscribe at any time. We will update this page first.

Sharing your child's artwork with us

We love seeing your little artist's work. Sharing is always optional. When a parent or legal guardian tags @LookAndSeeColorMe or uses #LookAndSeeColorMe on Instagram or another social network:

  • You confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian of the child, or have their permission, and that you took the photo.
  • You give Look & See Books free, non-exclusive permission to repost or share the photo, show it in our Little Artists Gallery on this website, and use it in our social media and promotional content about our books, with credit to your account.
  • You still own your photo. We will never sell it or license it to anyone else.

How we protect children when we share:

  • We feature the artwork and little hands. We will only show a child's face if the parent says yes to us in a private message first.
  • We use a first name and age at most (for example, "Sofia, 5"). Never a last name, school, city or anything that could locate a child.
  • We remove location data from photos before posting, and we crop out anything personal we notice, like name tags, house numbers or school uniforms.
  • We never contact a child directly. We only talk to parents.
  • We do not repost photos from accounts that appear to belong to a child.

Changed your mind? Send us a message on Instagram or email us, and we will take the photo down from our website and our accounts as soon as possible, usually within 72 hours. Reposts already shared by other people are outside our control, but we will remove everything we published.

Free printables

Our sticker sheets and coloring pages are free for personal, home and classroom use. Please don't sell them or share the files on other websites. Link to this page instead.

Visitor statistics (no cookies)

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter, to see totals: how many people visit, which pages they open, which website or app sent them (for example Instagram or the QR code in our book) and how many times buttons like "Buy on Amazon" or "Download" are clicked. It uses no cookies, does not follow you to other websites, does not build a profile of you, and does not tell us who you are.

Links to other websites

Buttons like "Buy on Amazon" and "Open Instagram" take you to websites we don't control, each with its own privacy policy. Grown-ups, please be with your child when they leave this page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
